About Butterfield Bakery

Our Story

Butterfield Bakery is a proud independent bakery based in the Small Street Mall in Pretoria Central. For years we have been serving the Pretoria CBD community with freshly baked bread and pastries, becoming a familiar and trusted stop for commuters, office workers and families alike.

Every loaf and pastry that leaves our kitchen is baked fresh on the premises, using time-tested recipes and quality ingredients, so that every visit feels like a small treat.

Our Mission

To bring freshly baked, affordable and delicious baked goods to the Pretoria Central community every single day, served with warmth and genuine care.

Our Vision

To be the most loved neighbourhood bakery in Pretoria Central, known for consistent quality, friendly service and a welcoming, accessible space for everyone who walks through our door.

Who We Serve

We serve commuters passing through Pretoria Central, office workers on their lunch break, families looking for celebration cakes, and anyone nearby craving something freshly baked. Our accessible location and parking make us an easy stop for customers of all ages and abilities.

Our Team

Our small, dedicated team of bakers and front-of-house staff arrive before sunrise to make sure the shelves are full of fresh bread and pastries by opening time. Our friendly staff is always ready to help you find the perfect treat for any occasion.
